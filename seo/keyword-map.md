## National BOFU insight pages

`/insights/...` pages own research and buying-decision modifiers such as cost, worth-it, comparison, budget and how-to-choose. They support commercial pages rather than replacing them.

Examples:

- `/insights/restaurant-marketing-agency-cost-uk` owns `restaurant marketing agency cost UK`.
- `/insights/restaurant-marketing-agency-vs-freelancer` owns the agency-vs-freelancer comparison.
- `/insights/restaurant-seo-cost-uk` owns restaurant SEO cost intent, while `/restaurant-seo-agency` owns supplier/hire intent.
- `/insights/restaurant-social-media-management-cost-uk` owns cost intent, while `/restaurant-social-media-marketing-agency` owns agency/service intent.
- `/insights/pub-marketing-agency-cost-uk` owns pub agency cost intent, while `/pubs-bars` owns the broad pub marketing agency term.
- `/insights/drinks-marketing-agency-cost-uk` owns drinks agency cost intent, while `/drinks-brands` owns the broad drinks marketing agency term.
- Cross-hospitality cost/comparison guides under `/insights/` support the matching `/services/...` page.

## Local restaurant insight cluster

The local article library contains Manchester/Greater Manchester and major UK-city pages for modifiers such as `affordable`, `best`, `prices`, `premium`, `quality`, and local SEO.

Ownership rules:

1. `/restaurant-marketing-agency-manchester` owns the broad `restaurant marketing agency Manchester` commercial term.
2. Manchester insight pages must retain their modifier, for example `affordable`, `best`, `prices`, `packages`, `premium`, `quality` or a specific service such as SEO.
3. Greater Manchester town/borough pages own their named locality plus their modifier. They must not be rewritten as generic Manchester pages.
4. Liverpool, Leeds, Birmingham, Bradford, Sheffield, Bristol, Newcastle, Nottingham, Leicester, Cardiff, Glasgow and Edinburgh insight pages own the city-specific modifier expressed in their slug and page title.
5. If a future city receives a dedicated root-level flagship money page, review all existing `/insights/` pages for that city and make the flagship the owner of the unmodified agency head term.

## Internal linking hierarchy

Preferred journey:

`Local/modifier insight -> relevant specialist or flagship money page -> pricing/contact`

`National BOFU guide -> relevant money/service page -> pricing/contact`

`Money page -> tightly relevant guides -> pricing/contact`

Use descriptive anchors, but vary wording naturally. Do not force the same exact-match anchor across every page.

## Publishing guardrails

1. Every indexable page gets one primary intent and one clear owner relationship.
2. Do not create a second page for the same query merely because a slightly different slug is available.
3. Before creating a new restaurant service page, check the root-level restaurant specialist list above.
4. Before creating a new city page, check all existing `/insights/` slugs for that city and decide whether the new page is a flagship or a modifier page.
5. Cost, comparison, worth-it and how-to-choose queries generally belong in `/insights/`; supplier/hire/service queries generally belong on commercial landing pages.
6. `/pricing` owns No Quiet Nights package prices. General market-cost queries belong to the relevant insight guide.
7. `/restaurants`, `/pubs-bars` and `/drinks-brands` own their broad sector head terms.
8. Do not add `<meta name="keywords">`. Keyword ownership is expressed through page purpose, metadata, headings, copy, schema and internal links.
9. Every indexable page should have a self-referencing canonical on `https://www.noquietnights.co.uk` and a route into the commercial site architecture.
10. When a rich flagship page replaces an older shallow page for the same intent, improve the existing URL rather than publishing a competing URL.
