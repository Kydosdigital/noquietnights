Straight answers

Before you
ask.

Anything genuinely specific belongs in your proposal, where it cannot hide in vague website language.

01Is £165 really enough to get started?+

Yes. Starter is deliberately focused: three planned posts per week across the agreed Instagram, Facebook and TikTok plan, supported by design, editing, stories and a content calendar. It solves consistency before adding infrastructure.

02Do you film content at our venue?+

Professional graphic design and video editing are included. On-location photography or filming is scoped separately when new asset capture is needed, so you know the cost before work starts.

03Is the advertising budget included?+

Paid ads management is included in Pipeline and Partner. The budget paid to Meta, Google or another platform is separate and remains yours.

04Can you guarantee more bookings or revenue?+

No responsible studio can guarantee a commercial result. Performance also depends on the offer, location, pricing, capacity and guest experience. We agree the measurable goal, instrument the journey and report what the activity can prove.

05Which website plan includes bookings or online sales?+

Pro includes e-commerce or booking functionality. Starter and Growth can link cleanly to an existing external booking destination within the agreed scope.

06Are there contract or cancellation conditions?+

The service period, payment schedule, cancellation terms and exact deliverables are set out in the proposal before work begins. This page describes the offer; the signed scope defines the engagement.
