Plain-English privacy

Your details are for the conversation.

Last reviewed 28 August 2026

01

What this site collects

The project brief is composed in your own email application. The site itself does not store the form answers. If you send that email, the receiving business will hold the contact details and information you chose to include.

02

Why the information is used

Enquiry information is used to review the fit, respond to the request, prepare a scope and keep a record of the commercial conversation where necessary.

03

Analytics and cookies

This release does not install advertising cookies or third-party analytics by default. If measurement tools are added later, this notice and the consent experience should be updated before they go live.

04

Your choices

You can ask for access, correction or deletion of enquiry information by emailing hello@noquietnights.co.uk. Legal, accounting or contract records may need to be retained where the law requires it.

05

Before public launch

The final legal entity, registered address, data controller contact and retention schedule must be inserted once the operator supplies them. This page is a practical starting policy, not legal advice.
